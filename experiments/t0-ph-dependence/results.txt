========================================================================
#25 - cost of holding t0 fixed when it is pH-dependent
========================================================================

Reported swing: t0 0.83 -> 0.65 min (-21.7%) up to pH 11

1. Sensitivity of k to a t0 error, by how retained the solute is
   d(log k) for a -10% error in t0:

          k    tR/t0   d(log10 k)
       0.05     1.05        0.523
       0.10     1.10        0.347
       0.30     1.30        0.171
       1.00     2.00        0.087
       5.00     6.00        0.054
      20.00    21.00        0.048

   -> the error explodes as k -> 0. An ionised acid at D=1.5 and
      k_neutral=10 sits at k=0.32, i.e. right in the steep region.

2. Bias in fitted D, sweep grid pH 2.5-6.5, t0 measured once at pH 2.5
   (silanol pKa is not a single number; scanned)

     pKa_sil  t0(2.5)  t0(6.5)   drift  D_fixed  D_percond  t0 bias
         4.5    0.828    0.652 -21.3%     2.12       1.43    +0.69
         6.0    0.830    0.693 -16.5%     1.85       1.43    +0.42
         7.5    0.830    0.814  -2.0%     1.46       1.43    +0.03
         9.0    0.830    0.829  -0.1%     1.43       1.43    +0.00

   (D_percond differs from 1.50 by ~-0.07 for ALL rows - that is the
    estimator's incomplete-plateau artifact, not a t0 effect, so it
    cancels in the difference.)

3. Same bias against the errors #15 says we must beat
   (worst case above, and the sweep's own measurement floor)

   t0 held fixed (silanol pKa 6.0)             0.4163
   LSER lack-of-fit floor, ACN (#15 s.5)       0.0300
   LSER lack-of-fit floor, MeOH (#15 s.5)      0.0410
   sweep's min detectable per-run dD (n=8)     0.0075
   prior SD on D_run (slice.py)                0.3000

   -> the t0 bias is 14x the LSER lack-of-fit floor and
      56x the difference the sweep is designed to resolve.

4. With a t0 marker in EVERY run (the protocol's mitigation)
   silanol pKa 4.5: t0-attributable bias falls from +0.69 to 0.00
   silanol pKa 6.0: t0-attributable bias falls from +0.42 to 0.00
   -> measuring t0 per condition removes the t0-attributable bias.
      The sweep is safe. The PRODUCT is the exposed case: a user
      predicting at pH 8 has not injected a t0 marker at pH 8.
